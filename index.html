What Is Straddle EA?

Straddle EA is an automated trading Expert Advisor designed for MetaTrader 5 and configured for XAUUSD (Gold) on the M15 timeframe. It uses pending Buy Stop and Sell Stop orders, configurable lot sizing, and trade-management controls according to its settings.
