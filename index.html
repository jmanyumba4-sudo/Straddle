Important Considerations

Understanding structural constraints is essential for proper evaluation.

  • ▲ Slippage During Major Releases: In periods of rapid liquidity depletion, order fills may deviate from requested levels due to natural market dynamics.
  • ▲ Spread Widening at Rollover: Overnight session changes often trigger significant spread expansion, which may impact tight boundary configurations.
  • ▲ Network & Execution Dependencies: Sub-optimal internet connections or server lag can degrade execution timing. A stable VPS remains necessary.
  • ▲ Varying Broker Feeds: Different liquidity providers stream distinct price feeds; results observed on one server will not identically replicate on another.
